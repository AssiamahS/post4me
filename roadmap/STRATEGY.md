# @sl.ysl.yy — 6-Month Reels Roadmap

_Prepared 2026-08-25. Faceless, one reel per day, voiceover + kinetic captions on a dark card, original audio only._

---

## 1. Positioning

@sl.ysl.yy is the go-to for **the record behind the record** — the samples, breaks, DJs and crate stories that explain *why* 90s/00s rap sounds the way it does, told by a working DJ who still plays those breaks in hip-hop, amapiano and jersey club rooms every weekend. The competitors are newsrooms; this account is a booth. **Okayplayer** does editorial culture and interview clips (its reels are artist soundbites, its comment-heavy posts are news carousels). **HipHopDX** wins on volume (3.8 posts/day) and album-anniversary nostalgia clips of artists talking. **Complex Music** trades on celebrity access (a 1.4M-play VERZUZ clip) and cover stories. **XXL** is news, Freshman-class legacy and courtroom drama. **DJ Mag** is electronic-first and proves the format that fits us — "story behind the hit" and technique tips both post above its average — but never touches rap. None of them dig, none of them explain a break from behind the decks, and none of them can be a real person you could book. That gap — hip-hop history *from the DJ's side of the table*, one card a day — is the position.

## 2. Content pillars (5 recurring series)

### SAMPLE SOURCE
- **Promise:** In under a minute you'll know the original record a classic was built from, who flipped it, and what they changed.
- **Hook formula:** `[Classic you know] is really [obscure record you don't] — here's what [producer] did to it.`
- **Example hook:** "Every head knows *C.R.E.A.M.* Almost nobody knows it's a 1967 Charmels record playing underneath. RZA changed three things."
- **Ideal length:** 35–50s (one source, one flip, one "what changed").
- **CTA style:** Save-driven. "Save this before you go pull the original." Ask for the *next* flip in comments ("what should I break down Thursday?").
- **Audio note:** No licensed audio, so the card *describes* the flip — chop points shown as text on a waveform graphic, pitch/tempo shifts stated ("slowed, pitched down a step, looped from bar 3"). Viewers go pull it up themselves — that's the save.

### THE BREAK
- **Promise:** The life story of one drum break — the original record, the first DJ to cut it, the rap records built on it, and where it lives now (jersey club, amapiano edits, drill).
- **Hook formula:** `This [X]-second drum break is in [N] records you know. It started here.`
- **Example hook:** "Four bars of drums from a 1972 Incredible Bongo Band record. Herc looped them in the Bronx. Fifty years later I'm still cutting them at 2am."
- **Ideal length:** 45–60s (lineage needs the room; this is the series that proves the DJ angle).
- **CTA style:** Lineage debate. "Name a record on this break I missed." Pin the best answer.

### CRATE NOTES
- **Promise:** One record every head should own — why it matters, what to look for on the label, what it goes for, and how a DJ actually uses it.
- **Hook formula:** `If you only own one [artist/label/year] record, it's this one — and here's the pressing to look for.`
- **Example hook:** "Ninety percent of people who say they love Pete Rock have never held this record. Here's what to look for on the label."
- **Ideal length:** 30–45s.
- **CTA style:** Share + collect. "Send this to the one friend who still buys vinyl." Rotate a "your crate" comment prompt: "what's the record you'd never sell?"

### DJ CRAFT
- **Promise:** One real technique, gear decision or set-building move from a working DJ — the stuff that only comes from playing rooms, not watching tutorials.
- **Hook formula:** `[Thing DJs get wrong] — what I actually do instead.`
- **Example hook:** "Most DJs drop the 90s record and lose the amapiano crowd in eight bars. Here's the transition that keeps both rooms."
- **Ideal length:** 30–45s (technique) / 50–60s (set-building).
- **CTA style:** Question-back to DJs. "What's your go-to bridge from hip-hop into club? Drop the BPM." Occasionally "follow for Saturday's set breakdown."

### ON THIS DAY
- **Promise:** The verified anniversary that matters today — album drop, first single, a DJ milestone — with the one detail the other pages leave out.
- **Hook formula:** `[N] years ago today, [record/event] — and the part nobody posts about.`
- **Example hook:** "Thirty-two years ago today *Illmatic* dropped. Everyone posts the cover. Nobody talks about the Joe Chambers record Premier had to find first."
- **Ideal length:** 25–40s (fastest series — competes with news pages on the day, wins on the detail).
- **CTA style:** Nostalgia comment. "Where were you when this dropped?" High comment velocity on the day; keep captions short.

## 3. Weekly cadence

| Day | Series | Why here |
|-----|--------|----------|
| Mon | **SAMPLE SOURCE** | Save-heavy content on the lowest-noise day; sets the week's flagship. |
| Tue | **THE BREAK** | Long-form lineage when watch-time is stable. |
| Wed | **DJ CRAFT** | Mid-week, DJs are prepping weekend sets. |
| Thu | **SAMPLE SOURCE** | Second slot; picks the comment-requested flip from Monday. |
| Fri | **CRATE NOTES** | Record-store / payday energy; share-driven. |
| Sat | **DJ CRAFT** | Second slot; set-building / gig-night angle, posted before doors. |
| Sun | **ON THIS DAY** | Floating slot — if a real anniversary lands mid-week, ON THIS DAY takes that day and the displaced series moves to Sunday. |

Two-slot series: **SAMPLE SOURCE** (the flagship, highest save rate) and **DJ CRAFT** (the authenticity engine and the one brands will buy into). The "REWIND / DAILY" quote series stays on stories and never gets a reel slot — it's the daily touchpoint, not the feed.

## 4. Hook rules

Derived from what performs in the research set (top posts by engagement per account).

1. **Lead with the record, not the person.** Our lane isn't celebrity access (Complex's 1.4M "Donell VERZUZ Joe" is unreachable for a faceless account). The record is the celebrity. First words on the card are a title every head recognises.
2. **Anniversaries are a guaranteed floor.** HipHopDX's "Not one, not two, but three iconic albums" and "A special album" reels sit at 146k–200k plays; XXL's "Ten years later" Freshman look-back is its #3 reel. Every ON THIS DAY hook starts with the number of years.
3. **"The story behind" beats "the fact about."** DJ Mag's best reel (90k plays, 2× its average) is "shares the story behind his chart-topping global hit." Every SAMPLE SOURCE and THE BREAK hook promises a story, not a stat.
4. **Withhold the payoff for one beat.** Okayplayer's "wasn't expecting to get THIS co-sign" — the hook names a reveal without giving it. Card 1 states the mystery, card 2 pays it.
5. **Count things.** "Not one, not two, but three," "four bars," "in 40 records you know." A number in the first line is a promise of structure and makes the caption scannable on mute.
6. **Ask a question people already have an opinion on.** Yung Miami's "Who y'all think is on the remix?" pulled 160 comments on a low-like post; Okayplayer's Palantir post got 1,506 comments on 3 likes. Comments come from opinions, not from admiration — every reel ends on a debatable prompt (best pressing, missed record, where-were-you).
7. **Craft content performs even when it's not the biggest.** DJ Mag's "tips for the ultimate kick" cleared 19k plays on a 44k-average account with a nobody-name artist. Technique hooks that promise a fix ("what I do instead") don't need a famous name.
8. **Sing-along nostalgia is an emotion, not a lyric.** HipHopDX's "Some songs you just have to sing along to" reel (134k plays) works on the *feeling*. We can't play the song, so the caption names the feeling: "the record that clears the bar at 1:55am."

## 5. Six-month arc

### Month 1 — September 2026: Consistency + baseline
- **Theme:** Prove the format. 30 reels, all five series live, zero missed days.
- **What shifts:** Nothing yet — the job is a clean baseline. Lock the card template (font, colour, tag position), voiceover pace, and a single caption structure.
- **Milestone:** 30/30 posted. First 500 followers. Identify the top-3 reels by saves (not plays).
- **Test:** Length. Run SAMPLE SOURCE at 35s vs 50s; DJ CRAFT at 30s vs 45s. Read avg-watch-% not plays.

### Month 2 — October 2026: Hooks + retention
- **Theme:** Rewrite the first 2 seconds of everything based on Month 1 retention curves.
- **What shifts:** Move to the "withhold one beat" structure (rule 4) across all series. Introduce the on-screen series tag as a hard brand element.
- **Milestone:** 1,500 followers. Avg watch-% up 15 points on the flagship series. First reel past 25k plays.
- **Test:** Hook type A/B on the same record — "record-first" vs "number-first" — same week, same series.

### Month 3 — November 2026: Comments + community
- **Theme:** Turn viewers into heads who argue. Every reel ends on a debate prompt; pin the best reply within an hour.
- **What shifts:** Thursday SAMPLE SOURCE becomes explicitly *viewer-requested* ("you asked Monday, here it is"). Start a monthly "your crate" story roundup from comments.
- **Milestone:** 3,000 followers. Comments/reel median ≥ 25. First collab reel with a record store or another DJ page (shared credit, original audio).
- **Test:** Poll-driven vs open-question CTAs — which converts to more replies.

### Month 4 — December 2026: Proof + media kit
- **Theme:** Package the numbers. Build the media kit (section 6) from three months of real data.
- **What shifts:** DJ CRAFT gets a gear-honest edge ("what I actually carry to a gig") — this is the series brands will eventually buy, so it needs to exist un-sponsored first. Holiday crate-gift angle on CRATE NOTES.
- **Milestone:** 5,000 followers. Media kit v1 done. First outreach to 5 small brands (affiliate or product-for-post, no fee).
- **Test:** Series-only weeks — one week where every reel is SAMPLE SOURCE or THE BREAK — to measure whether format focus lifts follows.

### Month 5 — January 2027: First revenue
- **Theme:** Convert. Land the first paid or affiliate integration inside DJ CRAFT without changing the voice.
- **What shifts:** One sponsored slot per week max (Saturday DJ CRAFT), labelled clearly. Everything else stays untouched.
- **Milestone:** 8,000 followers. One paid or affiliate deal live. Sponsored reel's watch-% within 10 points of un-sponsored average (if it's worse, the integration is wrong, not the audience).
- **Test:** Integration style — "the tool I used to make this transition" (native) vs a 5-second end-card (bolt-on). Keep whichever holds retention.

### Month 6 — February 2027: Scale what worked
- **Theme:** Double down, cut the rest. Retire the weakest series slot and give it to the best-performing one.
- **What shifts:** Re-derive the cadence from six months of data. Draft the next 6-month arc with a signature format (e.g. a monthly long-form THE BREAK "lineage map").
- **Milestone:** 12,000 followers. Rate card v2. Two brand relationships in conversation. Repost/clip strategy for YouTube Shorts and TikTok using the same faceless cards.
- **Test:** 60–90s "lineage" reels vs 45s on THE BREAK — does the DJ-story depth earn the longer watch.

_Follower milestones are targets to steer by, not forecasts. The real health metrics are save rate, avg watch-%, and comments per reel; if those are rising, follower count follows._

## 6. Monetization path

**When to approach:** Not before Month 4. Brands in this space buy *proof of niche*, not raw reach — three months of consistent posting, a clear series structure, and engagement data are the ask. Month 4 outreach is product-for-post and affiliate; Month 5 is the first paid conversation; by Month 6 you have a rate card built on your own numbers.

**How to approach:**
- Lead with the DJ, not the account. "I play hip-hop / amapiano / jersey club sets every weekend and run a daily reels series on sample history to X heads" is a pitch a gear brand's marketing lead understands in one line.
- Pitch the *series slot*, not a one-off: "Saturday DJ CRAFT, 4 reels/month, your product used on camera in the technique." Series buys are stickier and price higher.
- Start with small and mid-size brands where a reply is realistic (independent controller/accessory makers, reissue labels, record stores, DJ apparel), then use those as proof for the bigger names.
- Keep ad load at one sponsored reel per week max. The audience is here because it isn't a content farm — losing that loses the thing brands are paying for.

**What the media kit needs (one page, PDF + link):**
1. Handle, one-line positioning (section 1, shortened), and who runs it (working DJ, genres, city).
2. Audience: followers, monthly reach, monthly plays, avg watch-%, save rate, comments/reel, audience age/gender/top-5 cities (from Insights).
3. The five series with one screenshot and one number each.
4. Top-3 reels by saves and by plays, with the number on the image.
5. Formats offered: series slot integration, dedicated reel, story set, "gear I carry" feature, event pre/post coverage.
6. Rate card (Month 5 onward) — otherwise "rates on request."
7. Past collabs (Month 3+).
8. Brand-safety line: original audio only, no drama, no invented facts, sources credited.
9. Contact.

**Categories that fit (in priority order):**
- **DJ hardware & accessories** — controllers, mixers, turntables, needles/cartridges, slipmats, headphones, cases. Natural fit for DJ CRAFT.
- **DJ / production software** — library management, key/BPM analysis, stem tools, DVS. Fits DJ CRAFT and SAMPLE SOURCE ("how I found the tempo of the original").
- **Vinyl: reissue labels, record stores, pressing/storage** — fits CRATE NOTES exactly.
- **Apparel** — DJ / hip-hop heritage brands, gig-wear. Faceless account, so this is product-in-frame or story placement, not outfit content.
- **Events & venues** — local hip-hop nights, record fairs, DJ workshops; also the account's own gigs.
- **Music education / sample libraries / break packs** — original-audio-compatible and on-theme.
- **Not a fit:** alcohol, betting, anything requiring licensed music playback, anything that needs a face.

## 7. Guardrails

- **Accuracy.** Every date, chart position, sample credit and quote is verified against at least one primary or reputable source (liner notes, WhoSampled, Discogs, label pages, artist interviews on record) before recording the voiceover. No sample credit is stated as fact if the source is "everyone says." If it's disputed, say "widely credited" and name the dispute.
- **No invented quotes.** Artist quotes appear only when they can be traced to a dated interview, and the reel names the outlet. Paraphrase is labelled as paraphrase.
- **No copyrighted audio.** Voiceover and original sound design only — no sample clips, no "just two seconds" of the break, no hums of the melody. The card describes; the viewer goes and listens.
- **Credit sources.** Producer, sampled artist, original label and year on screen for every SAMPLE SOURCE and THE BREAK. Collaborators and photographers tagged. Research sources in the caption when it's not common knowledge.
- **No drama-baiting.** No beef, court cases, deaths-as-content, or "he said / she said." The competitor data shows drama drives comments; it also drives the audience brands don't want to sit next to. Anniversaries are celebrated, not turned into rankings that exist to start fights.
- **No fake urgency or fake numbers.** Follower counts, plays and rates in the media kit are screenshots from Insights, never rounded up.
- **Stay a DJ.** If a post couldn't be said from behind the decks by the person who actually plays these records, it doesn't post. The daily quote series, the sets, and the reels are the same person.
- **Sponsored content is labelled** every time, in the reel and the caption, with no exceptions for "gifted."
